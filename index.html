How it's measured

Rate = trays ÷ (packer-hours × 60). Trays come from cases_packed × pack_per_case; packer-hours are the sum of packers across each day's hourly labour rows.

An hour with 4 packers logged counts as 4 packer-hours whether or not they worked the full 60 minutes.
