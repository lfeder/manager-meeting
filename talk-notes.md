## 9 — Hard Challenge, the awkward lettuce work week
Tuesday and Friday the lettuce house is dark, and about two-thirds of that crew comes across to the cucumber packhouse.
The cuke crew dips those days because Tuesday is their day off — only 8 of 20 are rostered anywhere.
Friday: 14.6 people for the second-fewest cases. Saturday runs the same borrowed share at 10.6 people and does fine — so it's the headcount, not the transfer.

## 10 — Lettuce pack productivity
1.63 trays per packer-minute through April, 1.27 from May on. Down 22%.
Packer-hours went the other way: 28.9 a day to 34.3.
Slower to fill the hours, or the same hours on better work?
Every one of these is a nudge — run the same question on your own area this month.
